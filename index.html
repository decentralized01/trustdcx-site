Security Architecture

Fort Knox-grade.
Zero compromise.

Every private key is protected by multiple cryptographic layers — all on your device. No exceptions.

🧠

BIP39 On-Device Generation

Your 12-word seed is generated locally using cryptographic entropy. It is never transmitted, never synced, never stored in plaintext.

⚗️

Argon2id Key Stretching

Passwords are stretched with Argon2id (PHC winner) before touching any key material. Brute-force attacks are computationally infeasible.

📱

Android Keystore TEE Binding

The master encryption key is hardware-bound to your device's Trusted Execution Environment — inaccessible even to rooted devices.

🌐

Zero-Server Architecture

No backend holds your keys or seed. Optional cloud sync uses ciphertext only — the server never sees plaintext under any condition.

FAQ

Common questions

Is TrustDCX truly non-custodial? ▾
Yes — 100%. Your private keys are derived from a seed phrase generated entirely on your device and never transmitted anywhere. TrustDCX has no server holding your keys, requires no registration, and has zero ability to freeze or access your funds.
What happens if I lose my phone? ▾
Your 12-word seed phrase is the master key to all your wallets. Write it down and store it safely offline. On a new device, restore TrustDCX using those 12 words — all your balances and addresses are automatically recovered. No account needed.
How does nominee inheritance work? ▾
Inside the encrypted vault you can designate nominees — typically family members or trusted individuals. Your recovery credentials are encrypted and associated with those nominees. When the time comes, they can access the vault using cryptographic proof — no lawyers, no probate, no platform dependency.
Which blockchains are supported? ▾
TrustDCX supports Bitcoin (BTC), Ethereum (ETH), BNB Chain (BNB), Litecoin (LTC), Dogecoin (DOGE), Tron (TRX), Solana (SOL), TON, USDT and USDC — plus all EVM-compatible networks including Polygon, Arbitrum, Optimism and Base. More chains are added regularly.
Is TrustDCX free? ▾
The app is completely free to download and use. Standard on-chain gas fees apply to all transactions (paid to network validators, not TrustDCX). A small platform fee may apply on token swaps — always shown clearly before you confirm the transaction.
How is my data protected? ▾
All sensitive data is encrypted with AES-256-GCM. Your encryption key is derived via Argon2id and hardware-bound to your device's Trusted Execution Environment (TEE) via Android Keystore. Even if TrustDCX's servers were compromised, they hold only encrypted ciphertext — never your keys or seed.
